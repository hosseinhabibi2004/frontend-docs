## The brief

Silk Road Rail sold tickets through a 9-step flow built for desks, not phones. Over 60% of bookings now started on mobile and nearly half were abandoned before payment.

## What we found

We watched 18 travellers book a ticket. Most stalled at seat selection: a map they could not read at the size of a thumb.

> We didn't need a prettier map. We needed to stop asking people to choose a seat before they knew the price.

## What we changed

- Price first, seat optional, after payment
- One screen per decision, with the total always visible
- Saved passenger details, one tap to reuse

## Results

| Measure | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Abandoned bookings | 46% | 31% |
| Median booking time | 4 min 10 s | 1 min 55 s |
| Mobile conversion | 2.1% | 3.4% |
