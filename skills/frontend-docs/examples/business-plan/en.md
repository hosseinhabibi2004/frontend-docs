# The opportunity

Every year, small food producers in the region ship millions of single-use jars and tubs. Verdant Loop replaces them with a **deposit-based reusable container network**: producers fill, shops sell, customers return, and we wash and redistribute.

::: note
Assumption: a container survives 40 cycles on average; the break-even is reached at cycle 11.
:::

## The problem we solve

Small producers cannot afford their own refill logistics, and shops lack a trusted return flow. Customers who want to avoid waste have no consistent option.

## Our solution

A shared fleet of standardized containers, a washing hub, and a return network of 120 partner shops by the end of year one.
