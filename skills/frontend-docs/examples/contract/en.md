This Agreement is made on 12 March 2026 between the Client and the Contractor named above.

## Scope of work

The Contractor will design and deliver the documentation toolchain described in Schedule A (the **Deliverables**) in accordance with the timetable agreed between the parties.

The Contractor shall perform the services with reasonable skill and care and shall keep the Client informed of progress at least once every week.

Changes to the Deliverables require written agreement of both parties; unapproved changes shall not entitle either party to additional payment.

## Fees and payment

The Client shall pay a fixed fee of EUR 7,050.00, payable in two instalments: 50% on signature and 50% on acceptance of the Deliverables.

Invoices are due within 30 days of receipt. Late payment bears interest at 5% per annum above the base rate.

::: note
Fees exclude VAT, which is charged at the statutory rate where applicable.
:::

## Confidentiality

Each party shall keep the other's **Confidential Information** secret, use it only to perform this Agreement, and return or delete it on request.

This obligation survives termination of the Agreement for a period of three years.

## Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 14 days' written notice. On termination the Client shall pay for work completed up to the termination date.
